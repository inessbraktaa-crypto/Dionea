Our mission

Enterprise‑grade security shouldn't be a privilege of large companies.

Large organizations employ entire departments to protect their email. Small organizations — SMEs, associations, public institutions — don't. Yet they are the most targeted, precisely because attackers know they have no one watching.

Phishield exists to close that gap. We build tools that run automatically, require no security expertise, and protect the organizations that can't afford a dedicated security team. Every product we ship is open, auditable, and designed for adoption by people who aren't security engineers.

We believe email security should be as reliable and unremarkable as electricity. You shouldn't have to think about it. It should simply work — quietly, in the background, for the people who need it most.

Operating principles

Three principles guide everything we build.

They keep us focused, honest, and useful to the organizations we serve.

01

Focus

We build one product at a time and do it well. No feature bloat, no sprawling suite — just a tool that solves a real, painful problem.

Under the hood

Six layers of automated analysis.

Every suspicious email is inspected across multiple layers — no manual expertise needed.

01

Email headers & metadata

Origin, routing, timestamps, and structural anomalies.

📨
02

SPF / DKIM authentication

Sender verification against published DNS records.

🔐
03

Suspicious links & URL reputation

Every embedded link checked against known threat feeds.

🔗
04

Sender IP & domain reputation

Historical and real‑time reputation scoring for every sender.

05

Message structure anomalies

Detects tampering, spoofing, and routing inconsistencies.

Full automatic pipeline
